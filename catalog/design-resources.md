## How to use these

1. **Benchmark first.** Before building any site, identify 3–5 best comparable sites and study the top ones. Never design in a vacuum.
2. **Ingredients, not checklists.** Pull a palette here, a section rhythm there. The finished site must read as its own design, not a re-skinned template.
3. **Audience rules.** Older audiences need large type (≥18px), high contrast, large controls, a visible phone number, minimal motion, and no busy image behind text. Mobile-first always.
